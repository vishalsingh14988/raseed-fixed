Raseed — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Raseed is a personal bill vault that helps you store, read and organise your purchase bills and track their warranties. This policy explains what information Raseed collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Account information: when you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address. We use these only to create your account and show your private vault.
  • Bills you upload: the bill files (PDFs and photos) you choose to upload, and the details read from them — such as product, seller, purchase date, amount, invoice and order numbers, and warranty period.
  • Optional Gmail access: only if you explicitly connect Gmail, Raseed reads bill and invoice emails (read-only) so they can be added to your vault. We never send email on your behalf.

How your bills are read

Text is extracted from your bills using OCR technology. Photos of bills may be processed by Google's Gemini AI service to read printed and handwritten text. Bill files are stored securely using Cloudinary. Details extracted from a bill are visible only inside your own account.

How we use your information

  • To display, search and organise your bills in your vault.
  • To calculate and show warranty status and expiry dates.
  • To operate and improve the service.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your bills or email content for advertising.

Data sharing

Your data is processed only by the services needed to run Raseed: Google (sign-in and AI bill reading), Cloudinary (file storage) and Render (hosting). Each user's vault is private to their account — no other user can see your bills.

Your choices

You can delete any bill from your vault at any time. To delete your account and all associated data, contact us at the email below and we will remove it.
